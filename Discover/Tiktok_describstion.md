## Key Insight

The strongest recurring theme is **Portuguese authenticity** — creators consistently frame Búzio as a genuine taste of Lisbon/Portugal in Copenhagen, paired with wine + snacks in a cosy Nørrebro setting.

"Hidden gem" is a powerful throughline for the whole brand perception. It reinforces why people share it at all — finding something secret and telling others about it is core to why these videos perform.
